We revisited Korčula after 12 years. Which seems kinda fairytale-like. Also, in 12 years, Chinese zodiac goes full circle. Year of the horse both times, though Korčula, or *Korkyra melaina*, reminds me more of a unicorn.

However, if I had to describe Korčula with only one word, it would be: **magical**. 

This probably correlates with the time of year - we arrived on 19th June, so before school summer vacation in Slovenia. Lack of crowds. Refreshing, crystal-clear waters, just for us. And that incredible smell of the Mediterranean.

![Picture](/assets/korcula2/lumbarda.jpg)
*View from the balcony in Lumbarda*

The timing could not be better for a different reason as well.

It correlated with heat-wave, which devastated throughout Europe, but only brushed southern Dalmatia. It lasted fewer days, didn't overpass 35 degrees Celsius, and since we were in Lumbarda at the time, *maestral* kept us cool at all times. Also, we were both near the sea and the pool.


After 8 days in Lumbarda, we moved to the south of island, near Prižba. It was notably hotter here and less windy overall, but we were only a couple of steps from a private beach, so it was convenient to stay on the beach with its pleasantly cooling *povjetarac* (gentle breeze), or just go swimming at 11 pm to cool down. I even saw a falling star while swimming in the sea!

![Picture](/assets/korcula2/prizba.jpg)
*View from the balcony in Prižba*

Apart from beautiful blue hues of the sea and very green and forresty inlands, we noticed a lot more butterflies (and some cats, to our offspring's delight) than we would at home, most notably swallowtails, *lastovičarji*.

![Picture](/assets/korcula2/metulj.jpg)



We managed to mostly steer clear of the overpriced fried squid, instead we carefully selected two restaurants with favourable reviews - *Konoba Mate* and *Pagar*, both in the interior of the island. 

I ordered shrimp pasta in both, and can confirm Konoba Mate does live up to its expectations, while Pagar, in my opinion, was somewhat disappointing, though I don't doubt the freshness of their ingredients. 

So called "žrnovski makaruni", a type of endemic pasta, which were borderline gnocchi or *svaljki*, can be found elsewhere under a different name, for example in Krk as *šurlice*. 


We revisited old favourites:
 - Camp Vrbovica: This is where we stayed during our first visit to Korčula. The beach bar vibe is still awesome, only the prices have gone up (5 eur for an ice-cream?). The camp itself is the same size and well-maintained. I was tempted to stay there the next time (in 12 years from now?)

![Picture](/assets/korcula2/vrbovica.jpg)

- Vela luka:
I could not find my favourite souvenir shop no more, but the floor mosaics seem to have multiplied. Other than that, nothing had changed, really.

![Picture](/assets/korcula2/vela_luka.jpg)


![Picture](/assets/korcula2/muca.jpg)

Have a lovely remainder of the summer and keep cool!
